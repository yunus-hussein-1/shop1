↩️ Return & Exchange Policy

We understand that shopping online requires trust. That's why we have a clear return policy that's actually implemented from within your account — not just words on a page.

✅ When can you return or exchange a product?

  • Within 3 days of receiving the order.
  • If the product arrives damaged or doesn't match the description shown.
  • The product must be in its original condition and unused.

📝 How to request a return or exchange?

  1. Go to the "My Orders" page and select the completed order.
  2. Tap the "↩️ Return/Exchange" button next to the relevant product.
  3. Choose the request type (return or exchange) and write your reason.
  4. Our team will review your request and contact you as soon as possible.

💳 Refunds

After a return request is approved, we'll coordinate with you directly to refund the amount via Sham Cash within a reasonable timeframe. We commit to full transparency with you at every step.

🤝 Shared responsibility

The seller is responsible for the quality and accurate description of their products, and we are responsible for following up on any return request seriously until it reaches a fair resolution for both parties.
