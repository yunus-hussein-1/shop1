Your privacy isn't a footnote to us — it's a core part of our commitment to you. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and where it goes.

🗂️ What data we collect

  • Account information: your name, email, phone number, and date of birth (if you choose to add it).
  • Delivery addresses: so we can get your order to the right place.
  • Payment proof: Sham Cash transaction number or receipt photo — used only to verify your order, never shared with any third party.

🎯 How we use your data

We use it exclusively to: fulfil your orders accurately, improve your experience on the site, and contact you about your account or orders only. We never sell your data or share it for marketing purposes with any external party.

🛡️ How we protect your data

  • Your password is fully encrypted — not even our team can see it as readable text.
  • Access to your data is restricted to authorized members of the {{ brand_name }} team only.
  • We continuously review our security measures to keep them at their best.

✋ Your full rights

Your account is 100% yours. You can, at any time:

  • Edit your personal information from the "My Account" page.
  • Permanently delete your account and all its data from the Settings page — in one click.
  • Contact us for any further questions about your data.
